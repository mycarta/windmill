    How to Play

    In this game, the wind blows only from the 8 directions on the compass.

    Each turn:

    1. Tap an empty square to place a turbine.
    2. The wind blows from one direction on the compass, picked at random. Every turbine leaves a wake downwind, drawn as a pale streak. A turbine inside another turbine's wake is hit, and its square gets a red border. The wake passes with the gust, but the lost output stays: a turbine that has been hit changes colour.

    Each direction blows once. The game ends when every direction has blown. The record under the compass lists which turbines each gust hit.

    With 8 directions, Replay with 16 directions blows all 16 directions, one at a time, over the turbines on the board. It changes nothing in your game.

    Based on Dandelions from Math Games with Bad Drawings by Ben Orlin.
